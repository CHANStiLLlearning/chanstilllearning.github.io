Understanding the FIRE Movement and the 4% Rule

FIRE stands for Financial Independence, Retire Early. The foundational principle of FIRE is that once your invested assets can generate sufficient passive income to cover your living expenses for the rest of your life, working becomes optional.

The Trinity Study & The 4% Rule

Based on historical stock and bond performance over 30-year horizons, the Trinity Study concluded that an investor who withdraws 4% of their initial portfolio value in year one (adjusted annually for inflation) has a 95%+ probability of never running out of money.

How to Find Your FIRE Number (Rule of 25)

The Math: Your Target Portfolio = Desired Annual Living Expenses × 25

For instance, if you want to spend $40,000 per year in retirement:
$40,000 × 25 = $1,000,000 Nest Egg
